This policy covers personal information that may be provided to, or processed through, the Novex Foxter website. The information involved depends on how you use the site and which features you choose to use.

01

Information we receive

You may choose to provide information when you contact us, submit an enquiry, or apply for a role. Depending on the form and the information you choose to include, this may contain details such as your name, email address, message, or application materials.

When you browse a website, technical information such as your browser type, device, or pages visited may also be processed by the site’s hosting or other services. The information available depends on the services and features in use.

02

How information may be used

Information you provide may be used to respond to your request, communicate with you, consider an application, or operate and improve the website. We aim to use information in ways that are relevant to the reason it was provided and consistent with applicable requirements.

03

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to support site functionality, remember preferences, or understand how a website is used. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies may affect site features.

Sharing information

Information may be handled by service providers that support website hosting, communications, or other site operations, where applicable. It may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect legal rights and site security.

We do not describe specific providers or international data arrangements here because these can depend on the services in use. Please contact us if you need further information about a particular interaction.

05

Retention and security

Retention periods can depend on the nature of your request, operational needs, and applicable legal requirements. We take reasonable steps intended to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

06

Your choices and rights

You can choose what information to include when you contact us. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or limit certain processing.

To ask a question or make a privacy-related request, please contact our team ↗. We may need to verify your request and will respond as required by applicable law.

07

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the website, the services it uses, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
