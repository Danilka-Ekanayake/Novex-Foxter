We believe it should be easy to understand the technologies behind your online experience. This policy explains cookies and how you can manage them.

On this page 01 What are cookies? 02 How cookies are used 03 Types of cookies 04 Your choices 05 Policy updates
01

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store in your browser or on your device when you visit. They help websites remember information between pages or visits. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, can serve related purposes.

02

How cookies are used

Websites can use cookies to support core functionality, remember preferences, understand how visitors use a site, or support embedded content and campaigns. The cookies present may depend on the features and third-party services available on the site at the time of your visit.

03

Types of cookies

Cookies are often grouped by what they do. These are common categories you may encounter on websites:

Essential

Support core functions such as navigation, security, and access to requested features.

Site functionality

Preferences

Remember choices like language or settings to make future visits feel more familiar.

Remember choices

Analytics

Help site owners understand visits and improve how pages and features work.

Improve the experience

Marketing

May be used to measure campaigns or tailor advertising across services.

Campaign measurement

These are general cookie categories, not a list of specific cookies currently active on this site. A cookie’s lifetime may be limited to your browsing session or may continue until it expires or you remove it.

04

Your choices

You can usually review, block, or delete cookies in your browser’s privacy or site settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies or choose which sites can use them. If you block essential cookies, some parts of a website may not work as intended.
